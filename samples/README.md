# 40 automotive listing examples

Open [index.html](./index.html) in a modern local browser. The gallery runs from local HTML. Third-party thumbnail files are omitted from this public repository; use each card's original-source link to inspect the photo. The 40 observed records and editorial notes are in [listings.tsv](./listings.tsv). The 40 data rows can be edited and used to build a future gallery from authorized dealer media.

**Collection:** Autotrader search cards viewed 10 October 2026 in a Southwest Florida context. Exact source URL is attached to each card. Card prices, mileage, seller and inventory status are time-bound and may now differ. We did not inspect full detail text, VIN, equipment, condition, fees or history for every vehicle.

**Grade:** An editorial 0–100 card-presentation score, based on identity/trim clarity (25), price and mileage clarity (20), useful differentiators (20), visible photo evidence (20), and seller/next-step clarity (15). Because only search cards were observed, no score exceeds 72 and the grade must not be treated as an assessment of the actual vehicle or complete listing. The card-level suggestion explains the most useful next facts and photos to request.

**Copy:** Proposed paragraphs restate only the observed year/make/model/trim, advertised price, advertised mileage, seller and certification label where shown. The copy asks the buyer to confirm current terms, availability, equipment and condition. It does not invent options, accident history, warranty, dealer fees or vehicle quality. Dealer approval is required before use.

**Images:** The private research copy used unchanged 408×306 AVIF thumbnails for a reversible browser presentation preview. The public repository contains placeholders only. Production requires rights-cleared, high-resolution dealer originals. No videos were collected or altered.

**Rights:** Marketplace thumbnails are excluded from this public repository and must not be republished as dealer assets without permission. For production, use dealer-supplied media and destination-approved derivatives.
