Crankshaft dealer sales kit

Use with the proposed plan sheet and a dealer-specific scope. Crankshaft is the company. Listing Tuner is the managed listing service.

Plain-language pitch

“Crankshaft makes your existing vehicle listings more useful and handles the calls they generate. We improve descriptions and media from your actual inventory, answer inbound interest, verify what the shopper wants and connect qualified callers with your sales team. You keep your DMS, CRM, marketplace accounts and control of every price and material claim.”

Discovery questions

  1. Where do stock, price, status, photos and descriptions come from today? Which system actually feeds Autotrader, Cars.com, your site and other destinations?
  2. How many active vehicles and new/updated units each month? Who approves price, fee wording, condition and media?
  3. What footage exists per unit, who owns its rights, and what clip formats do your channels accept?
  4. Which inbound numbers lead to sales, service, parts and after-hours? How are calls assigned now and what happens when an agent does not answer?
  5. Which CRM receives leads, and can it accept an API/webhook or ADF/XML email with an acknowledgement?
  6. What are current answered-call, transfer, appointment and sale outcomes? Can the dealer share a baseline and disposition data?
  7. What is the dealer's approved recording notice, retention policy, enrichment source and consent process?

Demonstration sequence

Use two dealer-authorized vehicles. Show source facts beside the draft, then the original and proposed photo order/framing, then a short client-footage clip with the Jev candidate score and human-approved edit. Make a test call for a specific stock number, a sold vehicle, an after-hours caller and an unanswered transfer. Show the CRM receipt and usage ledger. State which integrations are live versus simulated in the demo.

Proposed plan framing

Launch is $795/month for 30 active listings and 750 AI verifier minutes. Growth is $1,295 for 60 active listings and 1,500 minutes. Scale is $1,995 for 100 active listings and 2,500 minutes. Each tier includes 25 AI minutes per active listing; rebuilt listing and clip allowances are in the business model. There is no separate minute, token or decision bill; dealers upgrade plans when they need more recurring capacity. The agreed human or callback route keeps callers from being dropped at the minute threshold. These minute allowances are a proposal pending pilot cost validation. Pilot: $495 for ten vehicles and limited test calls, credited toward month one within 30 days. Quote vendor certification or unusual connector work separately.

Proposal contents

Identify dealer legal entity, authorized inventory source, stock scope, destinations, listing and clip volume, included AI verifier minutes, upgrade and fallback rules, call number and transfer rules, CRM endpoint, enrichment policy, media rights, approval contacts, reporting, start date, pilot acceptance criteria, setup charges and exit/porting process. Attach the latest plan/pricing version and disclose that vendor accounts and marketplace rights remain dealer-owned.

Responses to common concerns

  • “Will AI invent vehicle facts?” We draft from source fields, block unsupported claims, keep an audit trail and require dealer approval of material content.
  • “Will photos hide flaws?” Originals remain untouched. Routine edits are framing/tonal only, and defect views stay in the set. The dealer approves final media.
  • “Does Jev edit the video?” Jev ranks candidate moments from processed footage. An editor cuts, captions, reviews and delivers the clip.
  • “What happens if the AI cannot verify or transfer?” It moves to a human or callback queue with the recorded reason. A transfer counts only after an agent answers or accepts.
  • “Will we see a separate minute or token bill?” No. AI verifier minutes, including the underlying token usage, are included in the plan; the dashboard gives early warnings and we propose a plan upgrade when usage grows.

Do not promise a conversion lift, platform ranking, specific CRM connector, accident-free history, all-in fees or a qualified-call percentage until those are evidenced for the dealer.
