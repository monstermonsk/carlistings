## Go/no-go for live use

Require a signed scope and rights, accepted stock mapping, one successful read and change reconciliation, one dealer-approved listing published and checked live, signed call routing/consent configuration, completed transfer and no-answer tests, confirmed CRM lead receipt, verified token/minute ledger and rollback/fallback owner. If a required item is missing, keep the affected path in test or manual mode.
