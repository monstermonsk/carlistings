Dealer questions

Straight answers.

Will AI invent vehicle facts or change the asking price?

No. Drafts come from dealer-authorized facts, material claims receive review, and the dealer approves price and publication.

Will photo work hide damage?

No. We preserve source images and visible condition, use modest framing and tonal treatment, and keep defect views in the set.

Does Jev generate a new vehicle video?

No. Jev ranks candidate moments in your footage. An editor makes and reviews cuts from the real vehicle video you supply.

What happens if the verifier is unsure or nobody answers?

The call follows your approved human or callback path. A transfer is marked successful only when the agent answers or accepts.

Will we receive minute or token charges?

No separate voice usage bill is planned. The monthly plan includes its proposed AI minutes, and growing dealers move to a larger plan. The pilot must validate final minute allowances.
